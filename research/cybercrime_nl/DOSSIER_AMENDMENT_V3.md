## Versioned changes

### Exact source passages for generated evidence

Keep every original title and body literally unchanged in the request. Add a
deterministic, complete, nonoverlapping catalog of source passages with short
wire IDs, field, offsets and exact text. Preserve source hashes and stable
identities outside the short labels. Catalog reconstruction must reproduce all
source characters, including whitespace, Unicode and line endings.

Luna summaries and Astra references select passage IDs instead of retyping
quotations or offsets. Expand only the selected evidence references into exact
original document/field/quote values, retaining their source offsets and catalog
identity. Preserve the raw request and response. Validate schema, catalog
binding, source coverage and exact quotations after expansion; reject unknown
IDs or missing required sources. This is mechanical source lookup, not correction
of claims or evidence fabrication. The independent full-source faithfulness
check remains mandatory. A selected passage can still fail to support a claim.

Include the existing event-identity and whole-article clarity rules explicitly in
the summary instruction. Prefer concise identity prose while preserving all
essential distinguishing facts, qualifications, contradictions and unknowns.
No output or source truncation is allowed to achieve brevity. The complete request
representation counts toward existing input capacity and cost reservations.

### Identity-aware assignment decisions and bounded packing

The inclusion question is whether evidence describes the same event. Retain
`P(same_event) >= 0.85` for a clear positive. Treat
`P(related_event) + P(different_event) >= 0.85` as a clear nonmatch: uncertainty
between these two labels does not itself imply uncertainty about event identity.
Other outcomes remain unresolved. Exactly one clear positive and no unresolved
competitor are needed; multiple positives remain a conflict. Whole-article
clarity still requires the original 0.85 threshold. These are operational,
uncalibrated probabilities, not a demonstrated accuracy guarantee.

Consider the same first five and recovery fifteen candidates. Pack up to fifteen
summary candidates into one request when the complete packet fits the existing
32,000-token proxy and byte limits; split deterministically when capacity requires
it. Preserve every candidate, immutable version and question. Combine clarity
and competitor evidence across all packets; never accept a partial candidate set
because a packet is missing. Do not merge conflicting dossiers automatically.

### Functional pilot with withheld article evidence

Select the next sixteen hash-ranked packets, excluding both earlier pilot packet
sets and all previously attempted summary-source articles. Selection is frozen
before the new pilot; failures are not replaced afterward.

From up to twelve deterministically hash-ranked newly verified groups with at
least two members, withhold one article from the summary evidence. Retain the
group identity and explicitly change its membership revision for this challenge.
The withheld article is then a query against the summaries. Its earlier full-text
pair judgment makes it a useful functional positive candidate, not independent
gold or a held-out scientific validation set. No summary receives its query's
source text. Also test up to 24 natural nearest candidates, ranked against groups
with usable summaries. Preserve the challenge and natural-query results separately.

Use the same pilot requirements: at least six valid same-event and clear-article
Astra references, no clear false merges, at least 90% usable seed summaries,
at least one accepted assignment reference, no failed seed packet and no new
unknown holds. Select reference samples deterministically and do not replace
technical or semantic failures. Reference reviewers receive full articles without
prior labels, retrieval scores or memberships. Repeated adaptive pilots do not
establish population precision or recall.
