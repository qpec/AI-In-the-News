## Targeted summary generation

Keep full original title/body inputs and the complete, exact v3 passage catalog.
Do not truncate sources, broaden citation granularity or repair model-authored
claims. Explicitly require atomic claims, with every clause supported by its
attached passage or passages. A fact elsewhere in an article does not validate
an incorrectly attached passage. Multiple passages may be selected as needed.

Dates of publication, statements and communications are separate from dates of
the incident or procedural action. Bind a date to an action only when the source
explicitly does so; otherwise preserve the unknown or qualification. Compatible
broad and precise counts, additional detail, and different reporting dates are
not contradictions. A contradiction needs incompatible claims about the same
referent, time and scope. Preserve allegations, attribution and uncertainty.

The model no longer reproduces opaque dossier and membership identifiers in its
summary output. The strict schema forbids those fields. After validation, insert
only the exact identifiers already bound in the request. Preserve raw output,
normalization version and hashes. This is metadata binding, never semantic repair.

## Explicit full-source quality review

Use a separate normal `gpt-6-luna` Responses request for every proposed usable
summary. Preserve its exact saved claims, source attribution and membership
version, and supply all full original articles. The reviewer receives no previous
labels, scores or diagnostic selection stratum. It returns `supported`,
`unsupported`, `identity_missing` or `uncertain`, a concrete reason, affected
claim paths and exact passage references, plus declared examination of every
source. Use the diagnostic's provider-compatible strict schema and full local
validation, including duplicate-path rejection and source coverage checks.

Only a mechanically valid `supported` result is usable. An unsupported claim
takes precedence over missing identity. Missing incidental details do not fail
an otherwise identifying summary. Source-reported allegations are assessed as
reported claims, not independently established world facts. Unknown outcomes,
capacity failures, incomplete coverage and uncertainty remain unusable.

This replaces the old probability-only summary gate for v4. It does not lower
or reinterpret the old threshold or change prior decisions. Explicit reasons
improve inspectability; they do not prove truth or eliminate correlated model
errors. Keep full-article seed and assignment Decisions and their existing
identity/clarity thresholds unchanged. Old summaries need valid v4 quality
review before reuse; versions explicitly rejected in the independent diagnostic
remain unusable. Never regenerate an attempted dossier/membership binding.
