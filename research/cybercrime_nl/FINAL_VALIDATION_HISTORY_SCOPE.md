## Required reconciliation

The protected scope manifest must identify every recorded study source selected
from this checklist, its exact owner/review identity, immutable head, declared
record/event/artifact scope, and the evidence for any exclusion. A missing owner,
unresolved path, unsupported record format or unlocalized source attribution
remains an explicit history gap. A verified main owner alone cannot establish
complete cross-study history. Follow source-owner, prior-owner and imported-view
links in retained plans until each recorded origin is reconciled or marked
unavailable. Deduplicate by origin plus physical attempt/review identity, not by
the number of copies or reports.

Match retained source versions to the final input index using exact input/title/
body hashes and documented source bindings. A different corpus label or document
ID alone cannot prove disjointness. Partial-text/card-only reviews retain their
actual evidence scope and can mark source-bound developmental exposure; they
cannot supply full-article pair truth. An unbound article mention supplies no
positive exposure claim and leaves its possible intersection unknown.

For `P`, count acknowledged developmental, diagnostic, reference and substantive
source review evidence. Routine production screening, ordinary extraction,
embedding, metadata-only diagnostics and source-free software tests do not by
themselves count. An operational pilot's exact development role must be established
from its registered use; the word `pilot` is not sufficient. Failed parsing does
not undo acknowledged source exposure, while a prepared packet alone does not
prove exposure. Provider-unknown transmission remains unknown.

Reference reuse is narrower than `P`: only an exact two-source current contract,
definition, role and trusted response provenance can supply a label. Old grouping,
screening, card, partial-source or incompatible-contract results cannot become
new pair labels. Preserve all physical attempted pair-reference roles when
applying the no-replacement rule, including unavailable results. Repeated
development on the same reference does not create independent judgments.

## Prospective task-history closure contract

This is an implementation requirement, not a completed discovery audit or a new
registered permission. Freeze its eventual concrete evidence before the final
statistical frame. The existing registered Study and its original seeds remain
unchanged.

`P` and reference-attempt history have different missingness. An unavailable
screening label may leave prior exposure uncertain even when the exact retained
question, request and dispatch prove an article-screening task. Such evidence
does not establish a previous pair-reference attempt. Conversely, an identified
pair-reference intent blocks same-role replacement even without a response,
usable label or acknowledged source exposure. Production pair comparisons and
whole-group QA must retain their actual task purpose; a pair appearing in them
does not automatically make them independent pair-reference judgments.

The finite discovery scope is the recorded studies in this checklist, their
retained source/prior/import links, and the recorded manual or conversational
reference tasks identified by that discovery. It is not universal human reading
or model pretraining. Unknown unrecorded reading cannot silently become an
invented model attempt. A known missing owner, unsupported possible-reference
format or unresolved recorded manual task cannot silently leave the scope.

The eventual audited discovery operation must retain:

- The exact checklist bytes, registered scope definition and source evidence for
  every root. A repository path or historical command is a discovery clue; the
  actual owner and immutable cutoff still require verification. In particular,
  the original event-pair/cross-window owner must be resolved using its retained
  result identity. `default_workspace()` also accepts an environment override,
  so the old script's default path alone cannot establish that identity.
- One evidenced disposition per root: an inspected owner/head, a verified copy
  of an already included physical origin, an exact source-disjoint or
  non-reference exclusion, or an unresolved gap. No corpus-name exclusion.
- Every retained owner/prior/import/manual-review edge found using bound
  producer-specific traversal rules, including its source record/artifact and
  terminal disposition. An unsupported link-bearing format leaves a gap.
- A complete census of durable generic model intents, including orphans, and
  separately recorded non-gateway/manual reference tasks. The classified set
  must equal that census; copied rows must resolve to the same original physical
  task rather than create independent judgments. Prepared-only requests remain
  distinct from attempts.
- Per-task exact evidence for one disposition: identified pair-reference
  attempt, proven other task purpose, exact-source disjointness, or unresolved
  possible pair-reference task. Unknown scope may be localized only when the
  retained task contract proves the complete affected inputs. An arbitrary
  article mention or a partial endpoint list in a batch cannot bound the rest.

Only the resulting concrete discovery graph and task census may derive a new
no-retry certificate. A supplied `complete` flag, a file hash, complete SQL row
counts, or a changed parent version cannot close semantic or discovery gaps.
Unresolved possible-reference regions retain their endpoint-local or global
blockers. P may remain unknown in the sampling strata after reference-task
history is resolved. The final frame and fixed initial slots must consume the
same frozen discovery/census artifacts and original seeds; changed scope later
requires an explicit amendment, without silent sample replacement.
