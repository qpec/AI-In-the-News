The fixed family is every monotone AND rule using the five retained embedding features and one of the existing publication gates (unrestricted, 30, seven or one UTC calendar days). ## Finite exhaustive existence argument

For any eligible box, let S contain **all** known-same development pairs it selects. There must be at least eight. Tighten each coordinate threshold to the minimum feature value among S, retaining the same publication gate. The tightened box retains exactly those known positives and can only remove other pairs. Thus known-same coverage, original positive weights, positive-article support and greedy disjoint-positive support remain unchanged, while observed and weighted pessimistic precision cannot decrease. Every eligible box therefore has an eligible tightened representative.

Enumerate every publication-eligible known-positive subset of size at least eight, form its five coordinate-minimum thresholds, and evaluate the resulting closure against **all** development references, including uncertain ones. Some generating subsets produce closures with additional positives; those are evaluated normally. With fourteen known positives there are at most 6,476 subsets per publication gate, or 25,904 generating boxes before gate restrictions and deduplication. Limits are one hundred development rows and fourteen known positives; exceeding a limit raises rather than truncates.

Retain every generating subset, threshold vector, publication gate and exact prediction-mask reference. Reuse metrics only for exactly identical selected-pair masks. Complete artifact hashes and required-artifact integrity anchors remain mandatory. An exhaustive no-rule conclusion requires complete features and references; insufficient positive support and processing failures are reported separately.

The [same selective requirements](EVENT_PAIR_PASSAGE_EXPERIMENT.md#registered-comparison) remain unchanged: observed and weighted pessimistic precision ≥0.90, observed and weighted known-same coverage ≥0.50, eight selected same references, twelve distinct positive articles and four greedy disjoint positive pairs. Rank eligible rules by weighted/observed coverage, weighted/observed precision, publication-gate preference unrestricted/30/7/1, then stricter threshold sum and lexicographic thresholds. Do not claim a simpler rule from this ranking.

The main diagnostic finds the highest minimum of observed/weighted precision among boxes already satisfying required coverage and positive support. This characterizes the tradeoff without silently lowering requirements. The overall precision diagnostic may fail coverage and is labeled accordingly. All five actual thresholds and the publication gate remain inspectable.

## Scope and validation boundary

If no representative qualifies, the supported conclusion is: **no monotone AND rule in this fixed five-feature/publication-gate family meets the unchanged development requirements on these model references**. This is not a conclusion about all embeddings, other classifier families, independently reviewed labels, population accuracy or event-dossier validity. It is not a held-out result. Model references are not human gold.

If a rule qualifies, freeze exactly one five-threshold candidate. No later variant selection is permitted. This offline operation launches no model or embedding calls, applies no tags and accepts no dossiers. The candidate remains unvalidated; a separate audited held-out execution is required before a pass can be claimed. The tightening argument applies to development eligibility only: tightening might remove nonpositive selections needed to meet validation's minimum of twenty sampled links, so it does not prove anything about held-out eligibility.

Before this final search is executed, a bounded exploratory fallback is also registered: if no rule meets the unchanged 90% development target, the **best-at-required-coverage/support** diagnostic may be frozen as a single hypothesis only when both observed and weighted precision reach 85%. This lower feasibility floor changes which hypothesis may receive one untouched held-out test, not the 90% development conclusion or the unchanged 90% held-out target. No other candidate is frozen, no retuning follows opening, and no tags/dossiers are applied or accepted. This fallback is declared before observing the final search's actual results.
