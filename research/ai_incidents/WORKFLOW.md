## Preparation for the next executed study

1. Freeze a new incident-only manifest that references original source/body and
   screening versions. Preserve all original 121,752 outcomes. Carry the 8,603
   keeps as the pre-clarity frame rather than claiming they are final eligibility.
2. Register a new operational clarity criterion and evaluation. Keep exclusions,
   ambiguity and technical missingness separate; never rewrite historic labels.
3. Audit the exact eligible source-to-vector intersection, representation
   compatibility, missingness and text/token sizes. Reuse existing vectors only
   with their retained lineage. Define new embedding work within the same USD20.
4. Implement and test the four processes, including full-text preservation,
   deterministic candidate queues, identity checks, immutable summaries,
   stale-result handling, accounting, unknown holds and no automatic retries.
5. Freeze the embedded development/held-out protocol and precise budget before
   dispatch. Preserve the budget required for summary generation and whole-frame
   coverage before optional deeper recovery. Reforecast the new summary method.
6. Execute through `Research.execute()` and the shared gateway in a separate
   protected owner; report stage coverage, actual costs and unresolved work.
7. Evaluate all stages against their reference sets, retaining sources for audit.
   Compare summaries with original evidence; treat a small model audit as limited
   evidence rather than validation of full event coverage.

The latest request organizes the research and its prospective replication; it
does not revive any expired/stopped historic extraction or screening execution.
The Netherlands cybercrime study has its own manifest, relevance question,
geographic rule, validation, budget and owner. Do not send its articles to the
incident-only owner or inherit AI relevance labels as cybercrime labels.

## Storage and reproducibility

Keep public methodology, code, tests and aggregate evidence indexes in Git. Keep
raw articles, identities, exact prompts/replies, credentials, vectors, working
databases and detailed reports in the existing protected stores outside Git.
Historical result hashes and owner names are recorded in the linked source
documents. A document copy or reorganization is not an audit replay.

Use the repository's [focused testing guidance](../../docs/TESTING.md). Software
fixtures verify implementation; corpus experiments and semantic references need
their separate registered research lifecycle. Synchronize the lean conversation
journal according to [workbench instructions](../../workbench/README.md); daily
reports remain private and do not replace research registration.
